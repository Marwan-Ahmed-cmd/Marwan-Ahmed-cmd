# 💫 About Me
Hi, I'm Marwan 👋  

Electrical & Electronic Engineering student working on **semiconductor device modelling** and **single-photon detectors for quantum communication**.

I build and validate avalanche-photodiode / SPAD device models in TCAD, alongside photonics simulations, embedded-systems projects, and physics-based mathematical models.

🔬 **Currently:** building a TCAD device-modelling portfolio — Si, GaAs, and InGaAs/InP APDs & SPADs — toward 1550 nm quantum-communication receivers.  
🎯 **Goal:** research & academia in optoelectronic device engineering.
